ABOUT AHBS INTERNATIONAL

WHO WE ARE

AHBS International is a professional industrial engineering and contracting firm providing practical, reliable and quality-focused solutions to the industrial sector in Pakistan. We work with industrial facilities that require dependable engineering services, professional project execution and attention to safety, quality and delivery schedules.

Our approach combines engineering knowledge, skilled manpower, organized project management and disciplined execution to deliver solutions that meet the operational requirements of modern industrial facilities. From individual engineering works to complete turnkey assignments, AHBS International aims to provide clients with a single, dependable engineering partner.
